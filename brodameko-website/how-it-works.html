Platform Guide

How BrodaMeko
Works.

One platform. Three user types. Every step explained for mechanics, sellers, and car owners.

Pick Your Role.
See Your Journey.

01

Download the Mechanic App

Get BrodaMeko Mechanic from the App Store or Google Play. It’s completely free to download and to join.

02

Create Your Profile

Enter your details, upload a profile photo, and describe your service specialties and areas of coverage.

03

Submit Verification

Upload your valid Nigerian ID and trade documentation. Our team reviews and approves within 24 hours.

04

Go Live on the Map

Once approved, toggle online. Your verified profile appears on the BrodaMeko map for car owners in your area.

05

Receive & Accept Job Requests

Car owners send job requests. You get a notification with job details, location, and the car issue. Accept or decline.

06

Navigate, Complete & Get Paid

Use in-app navigation to reach the customer. Chat if needed. Mark job complete. Payment is released instantly to your wallet.

01

Download the Seller App

Get BrodaMeko Seller from the App Store or Google Play. Free to download, free to join.

02

Register Your Business

Enter your shop name, contact details, and business location. Upload a shop photo and basic business information.

03

Business Verification

Submit your business ID and registration details. Our team verifies your shop within 24–48 hours.

04

List Your Parts Catalog

Add your parts inventory — photos, names, descriptions, and prices. Start with your fastest-moving items.

05

Receive Order Requests

Car owners and mechanics near you send order requests through the app. You get a notification with full order details.

06

Confirm, Fulfill & Get Paid

Accept the order, confirm availability, arrange delivery or pickup. Payment is released to your wallet on confirmation.

01

Join the Waitlist First

The car owner app is launching soon. Join the waitlist now to get early access when your area goes live.

02

Download & Open the App

When launched, download BrodaMeko from the App Store or Google Play. Open it — your location is detected automatically.

03

Describe Your Car Issue

Know the problem? Describe it and get matched to the right mechanic. Not sure? Book a diagnostics expert first.

04

Get Matched to a Verified Mechanic

BrodaMeko shows you nearby verified mechanics with ratings, specialties, and estimated arrival time. You choose.

05

Track in Real Time & Chat

Watch your mechanic’s location update live. Use in-app chat to communicate directly. No phone number needed.

06

Confirm & Pay Securely

Agree to the price before work starts. Pay through secure escrow. Release payment after you confirm the job is done.

Platform Features

Everything You Need.
Nothing You Don’t.

📍

Real-Time GPS Matching

Automatically finds verified mechanics and sellers within your area using live location data.

🔒

Escrow Payment

All payments are held safely until job completion. No one gets paid until the work is confirmed done.

💬

In-App Chat

Car owners and mechanics communicate directly through the app without sharing personal phone numbers.

🗺

Turn-by-Turn Navigation

Mechanics get in-app navigation to the customer’s exact location. No address confusion, no wasted time.

⭐

Ratings & Reviews

Both parties rate each other after every job. Quality is rewarded. Bad actors are removed from the platform.

💼

Earnings Wallet

Mechanics and sellers have a built-in wallet showing all earnings, deductions, and payout history in real time.

🛡

Dispute Resolution

Dedicated admin review for any job disputes. Evidence-based, fair, and fast — protecting both sides honestly.

📦

Spare Parts Marketplace

Car owners and mechanics can order verified auto parts from nearby sellers directly through the app.
