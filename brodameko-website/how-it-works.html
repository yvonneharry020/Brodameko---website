Platform Guide

How BrodaMeko Works.

One platform. Three user types. Every step explained for mechanics, sellers, and car owners.

Pick Your Role. See Your Journey.

01
Download the Mechanic App

Get BrodaMeko Mechanic from the App Store or Google Play. It’s completely free to download and to join.

02
Create Your Profile

Enter your details, upload a profile photo, and describe your service specialties and areas of coverage.

03
Submit Verification

Upload your valid Nigerian ID and trade documentation. Our team reviews and approves within 24 hours.

04
Go Live on the Map

Once approved, toggle online. Your verified profile appears on the BrodaMeko map for car owners in your area.

05
Receive & Accept Job Requests

Car owners send job requests. You get a notification with job details, location, and the car issue. Accept or decline.

06
Navigate, Complete & Get Paid

Use in-app navigation to reach the customer. Chat if needed. Mark job complete. Payment is released instantly to your wallet.

01
Download the Seller App

Get BrodaMeko Seller from the App Store or Google Play. It’s completely free to download and register your shop.

02
Register Your Business

Enter your shop name, address, and contact details. Upload a valid Nigerian ID and business information.

03
List Your Parts

Add your inventory — photos, part names, descriptions, and prices. No technical knowledge required. It takes minutes.

04
Go Live on the Marketplace

Once approved, your shop appears on the BrodaMeko map. Nearby mechanics and car owners will discover your parts first.

05
Receive Orders In-App

Buyers place and pay for orders directly through BrodaMeko. You get notified instantly with order details and delivery info.

06
Fulfil & Get Paid

Confirm the order, dispatch the part, and mark it as delivered. Payment is credited to your wallet after the 7-day verification window.

01
Join the Waitlist

The car owner app is coming very soon. Sign up for early access now so you’re first in line when it goes live.

02
Download & Create Account

Download the BrodaMeko car owner app. Register with your name, phone number, and email. Takes under 2 minutes.

03
Describe Your Car Issue

Tell BrodaMeko what’s wrong with your car — engine, tyres, electrical, or any other issue. Pin your location.

04
Get Matched Instantly

BrodaMeko finds the nearest verified mechanic in real time and sends them your request. See their profile, ratings, and ETA.

05
Track & Communicate

Track your mechanic live on the map. Chat directly through the app without sharing your personal phone number.

06
Pay Safely & Rate

Confirm job completion. Payment is released from escrow to the mechanic. Rate the service and help others find the best mechanics.

Platform Features

Everything You Need. Nothing You Don’t.

📍
Real-Time GPS Matching

Automatically finds verified mechanics and sellers within your area using live location data.

🔒
Escrow Payment

All payments are held safely until job completion. No one gets paid until the work is confirmed done.

💬
In-App Chat

Car owners and mechanics communicate directly through the app without sharing personal phone numbers.

🗺
Turn-by-Turn Navigation

Mechanics get in-app navigation to the customer’s exact location. No address confusion, no wasted time.

⭐
Ratings & Reviews

Both parties rate each other after every job. Quality is rewarded. Bad actors are removed from the platform.

💼
Earnings Wallet

Mechanics and sellers have a built-in wallet showing all earnings, deductions, and payout history in real time.

🛡
Dispute Resolution

Dedicated admin review for any job disputes. Evidence-based, fair, and fast — protecting both sides honestly.

📦
Spare Parts Marketplace

Car owners and mechanics can order verified auto parts from nearby sellers directly through the app.
