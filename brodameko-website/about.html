The Origin

Why We Built BrodaMeko.

Every driver in Lagos has a story. A car that broke down on the third mainland bridge. A mechanic who disappeared after collecting money. A spare part that turned out to be fake. A job that was “fixed” but failed again two days later. We got tired of hearing those stories because we lived them too.

BrodaMeko started from a simple conviction: the mechanic-to-car-owner relationship in Nigeria is broken at the trust layer. Not because mechanics are bad — most are skilled, hardworking people. But because there was no infrastructure to make them visible, accountable, and accessible in a way drivers could rely on.

So we built that infrastructure. A platform where mechanics are verified before they show up. Where prices are agreed before the job starts. Where payments are protected. Where records exist. Where good work is rewarded and poor work has consequences.

BrodaMeko is not an app. It is a trust system for the auto-services industry starting in Lagos, building toward every city in Africa and the rest of the world.

Our Values

What We Stand For. Every Single Day.

🔍
Radical Transparency

Every price, every fee, every deduction is visible. We believe opaque pricing is a form of dishonesty. BrodaMeko shows everything.

🤝
Mechanic Dignity

Nigerian mechanics are skilled professionals who deserve to be treated as such. We built this platform to elevate the trade, not exploit it.

🛡
Driver Safety

No car owner should fear being stranded or overcharged. BrodaMeko gives every driver a fast, safe, predictable way to get help.

📈
Local First

We are a Lagos-born platform solving Lagos problems first. We won’t expand until the foundation here is solid, trusted, and proven.

⚡
Speed as Respect

When your car breaks down, every minute matters. We treat response speed as a form of respect for the user’s time and stress level.

🧠
Data-Driven Quality

Ratings, response times, completion rates — we use real data to reward excellence and identify problems before they hurt users.
