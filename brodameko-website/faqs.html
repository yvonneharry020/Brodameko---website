Car Owners

Got a car question?
We’ve got answers.

Everything car owners need to know about using BrodaMeko to find mechanics and auto services near them.

BrodaMeko is Lagos’s on-demand auto-services platform that connects car owners with verified mechanics, auto technicians, and spare parts sellers near their location. Think of it like Uber but for your vehicle. When your car breaks down or needs servicing, simply open the app, describe the issue, and a vetted mechanic is dispatched to your location. Everything from booking to payment happens within the app.

Joining is simple. Visit brodameko.app/car-owner-join-waitlist, fill in your name, email, phone number, and location, then tap “Secure My Spot.” You’ll be among the first car owners notified the moment the app goes live. There are already over 1,840 car owners on the waitlist across Lagos.

BrodaMeko uses a secure escrow payment system. When you pay for a service, your money is held safely in escrow and is only released to the mechanic after the job is confirmed complete. BrodaMeko’s admin team will review both sides impartially, examine evidence, and issue a fair resolution within 48 hours. During this window, payment remains in escrow until the dispute is settled.

Spare Parts Sellers

Turn your shop into
a digital storefront.

Everything spare parts dealers and auto parts shops need to know about listing on BrodaMeko and receiving orders in-app.

BrodaMeko allows spare parts sellers to list their products on the platform and receive orders directly from mechanics and car owners who are physically near their shop. When a user searches for a part on BrodaMeko, nearby sellers appear first based on proximity. Orders are placed and paid for in-app, and sellers manage everything — listings, orders, payouts — from the seller dashboard.
There is no subscription fee or upfront listing cost to join BrodaMeko as a spare parts seller. Registration and listing are free. BrodaMeko only charges a commission of 5–15% on confirmed, completed orders. The exact commission rate depends on the product category. No sale, no charge. It’s that simple.
BrodaMeko charges a commission of 5% to 15% on confirmed completed orders for spare parts sellers. This commission varies by product type and category and is automatically deducted before your wallet is credited. There are no hidden fees, no monthly charges, and no upfront costs. Your full commission breakdown is always visible in your seller dashboard.
For spare parts sellers, payment disbursement occurs seven (7) days after the transaction is marked as completed on the platform. This waiting period allows BrodaMeko to verify the transaction, monitor for disputes, and confirm product authenticity and quality. After the 7-day window clears without a valid dispute, your wallet is credited and you can withdraw to your bank account.
Listing is straightforward. After downloading the seller app and completing registration, go to “My Products” in your dashboard and tap “Add New Part.” Upload clear photos of the part, add the part name, description, price, and available quantity. No technical experience is needed — the process is simple enough for any shop owner to manage themselves. Your listings become visible to nearby buyers immediately after being approved.
To register your spare parts business on BrodaMeko you will need:
  • A valid government-issued ID of the business owner
  • Your business name and address
  • Contact details (phone number and email)
  • Photos of your shop or products
Additionally, as a spare parts seller, you are required by BrodaMeko’s Terms of Use to retain verifiable proof of purchase or importation (invoices, import documents, or supplier receipts) for the parts you sell, available on request in the event of a dispute.
All spare part purchases on BrodaMeko come with a 7-day warranty period from the date the transaction is marked as completed. Within this window, buyers can raise complaints about the quality, authenticity, fitness, or performance of the part. As a seller, you are expected to respond to complaints in good faith, provide supporting purchase documentation, and cooperate with BrodaMeko’s dispute process. Selling authentic parts and keeping your invoices ready protects you completely.
Your buyers on BrodaMeko include both car owners who need specific parts for their vehicles and verified mechanics on the platform who need to source parts to complete jobs. This dual buyer base — retail and wholesale — means you can grow both your individual customer sales and your B2B mechanic orders from a single platform.
BrodaMeko uses GPS-based proximity matching to show buyers the nearest available spare parts sellers first. When someone searches for a part, they see sellers ranked by how close they are physically. This means that car owners and mechanics near your shop will discover you first — even without you spending anything on marketing. Being on BrodaMeko gives you instant local visibility to high-intent buyers in your area.
If a buyer raises a dispute within the 7-day warranty window, BrodaMeko will hold the payment in escrow and begin an investigation within 48 hours. You will be contacted to provide supporting documentation (invoice, import papers, supplier receipt) proving the authenticity and origin of the part. If you provide satisfactory evidence, the dispute is resolved in your favour and payment is released. Sellers who cannot provide documentation risk partial or full refunds to the buyer, penalties, or suspension.
